## What the extension does

During an online exam you start on [exam site], the Hawkeye extension helps the exam team
review the session fairly. It **flags moments for a human reviewer**. It never decides your
result, never blocks or ends your exam, and never identifies who you are by name.

## When it runs

Only after you accept the consent notice for a specific exam, and only until that exam ends. A
recording indicator is shown on the exam page while the camera and microphone are in use. If you
decline, nothing is captured.

## What is collected

- **Webcam:** a few still frames are sampled every 30 seconds and checked on your device for
  situations such as no face, more than one person, a phone or a book in view, or looking away.
  Frames are kept **only** when such a moment is detected (plus a few frames around it).
- **Microphone:** sound is checked on your device for speech. A short clip (at most 30 seconds)
  is kept **only** when speech is detected.
- **Browser events:** the type and time of events such as leaving fullscreen, switching tabs,
  copy/paste, refreshing or inactivity. **Never** page content, your answers, what you type,
  clipboard contents or web addresses.
- **Setup check:** whether your camera, microphone and device were ready (not part of your result).
- **Consent record:** your decision, the notice version (which also records whether you opted
  in to face matching) and the time.

The extension does not record your screen and does not record continuous video or audio.

## Optional face matching

> Keep this section only if [Organisation] turns face matching on in its policy. If it stays
> off (the default), replace this section with: "The extension does not use face recognition and
> does not create biometric templates."

[Organisation] may offer face matching for some exams. It runs **only if you tick a separate box**
on the consent notice; you can take the exam without it.

- **What it does:** when the camera starts, the extension takes a few extra photos and turns your
  face into a set of numbers (a face template). During the exam it compares the face in each
  sampled frame with that template, to notice if a different person seems to be at the camera.
- **Biometric data:** the face template is biometric data. It is created and used **only in your
  browser's memory**. It is never uploaded, never saved to disk and never sent to [Organisation]
  or anyone else, and it is deleted when the exam session ends.
- **What is uploaded:** if the face looks different in several sampled frames in a row, that
  moment is flagged for review with the flagged frame and one photo from the start of the
  session, so a reviewer can compare them. These are ordinary webcam frames, kept and deleted
  like any other evidence.
- **Limits:** face matching makes mistakes, more often in poor or very bright lighting, with a
  low-quality camera, or when your face is partly covered. A flag is never a finding; a person
  always looks at the photos, and it never decides your result.
- **Legal basis:** [your explicit consent (e.g. GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)) / other basis confirmed by
  legal review]. [Add any notice required by local biometric law, e.g. Illinois BIPA: purpose,
  retention schedule and destruction of biometric identifiers.] You can withdraw consent by
  contacting [e-mail] before the exam; the template only exists while the exam runs.
